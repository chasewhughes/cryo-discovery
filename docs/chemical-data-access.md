## Retrieval guidance for cryopreservation discovery

Use PubChem/ChEMBL to normalize compounds and gather mechanism/activity evidence; UniProt and Reactome to connect targets to pathways; BindingDB and RCSB PDB to add direct affinity or structural support; Cellosaurus to normalize cell models; and LINCS/CLUE to look for perturbational signatures. These resources are complementary and mostly observational: none alone proves that a compound preserves viability, membrane integrity, or function during a defined freeze–thaw protocol. Preserve source identifiers, assay conditions, cell-line identifiers, and literature citations in any downstream evidence table.
