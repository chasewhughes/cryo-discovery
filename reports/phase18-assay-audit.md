# Phase 18 CHEMBL1071707 assay audit

CHEMBL1071707 is an independent biochemical ROCK2 source associated with Morwick et al., DOI [10.1021/jm9014263](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm9014263). The primary article and ACS supporting information document a human ROCK2(1–543) routine luciferase assay with AKRRRLSSLRA peptide (500 nM), 750 nM ATP, 90 minutes at 28 °C, and luminescence detection. The SI separately documents an IMAP format at 100 µM ATP; those values must not be pooled with the luciferase endpoint. The article also reports luciferase detection-enzyme interference for some analogues, so this remains a readout-risk caveat.

The ChEMBL assay snapshot contains 51 records: 40 exact numeric IC50 records representing 39 unique molecules, 10 right-censored IC50 records, and one exact Ki record. Eleven exact records and ten right-censored records carry ChEMBL’s `Outside typical range` validity flag; flags alone do not establish unit errors. A material conflict exists for CHEMBL604249: activity 3131702 is flagged at 266,000 nM while activity 3131724 is unflagged at 92 nM, with identical structure and record ID. The article reports 266 nM for compound 33 and 92 nM for compound 34, so ChEMBL alone cannot determine the intended mapping. This molecule is held out pending source-table reconciliation; flagged rows are not selectively removed after seeing outcomes.

The frozen reconciliation maps 30 Table 4–7 analogue rows, documents 17 source-supported 1000-fold µM-to-nM corrections, preserves right-censoring, and excludes both ambiguous CHEMBL604249 records. The resulting curated panel is eligible for separately reported external evaluation and calibration under its frozen rules. Any evaluation must keep this assay separate from Phase 17 CHEMBL4328667 and must not support claims about CEPT biology, ice inhibition, or prospective efficacy.
